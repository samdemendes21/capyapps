CapyShape

A capybara that gets in shape with you.

Log food, water and workouts in seconds. The capybara fills up like a glass with the day's calories and changes shape as you train.

In closed testing on Google Play

How it helps

Easy calorie counting

Over 7,000 foods with everyday names. Tap, pick the portion, done.

Water in glasses

Eight glasses a day. Tap to fill, tap again to undo.

Workouts that count

Timer, weekly goal and burned calories added to your daily target.

A capybara that evolves

Five bodies, from cuddly to strong. It celebrates every win with you.

FAQ

Is CapyShape free?

Yes, with ads. Optional Premium removes ads, syncs watch workouts and unlocks full history.

Does my data go online?

No. Food, water and workouts stay on your phone. There is no account and no server.

Does it replace a dietitian?

No. Calories are estimates to help you day to day.
