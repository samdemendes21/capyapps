CapyShape privacy policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

CapyShape is a calorie, water and workout tracker with a virtual capybara. It works without an account and offline; signing in with Google is optional. It is for people 18 and over.

What the app stores: the capybara's name, starting body, goal, birth year (only for the 18+ check and the calorie suggestion), optional weight and height, and the foods, water and workouts you log. This data stays on your device and, if you turn on sync, in a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive. We have no server and receive none of it.

Sign in with Google, backup and sync (optional): in Settings › Backup and sync you can sign in with Google to use the app on more than one device. The app only receives your email (to show which account is connected) and the drive.appdata permission, which gives access only to a hidden CapyShape folder in your Drive; the app cannot see or change any other file. The device talks directly to Google; nothing goes through a server of ours. To stop, tap "Sign out". To delete the copy, remove CapyShape at myaccount.google.com › Security › Third-party apps, or delete the app's hidden data at drive.google.com › Settings › Manage apps.

Health Connect (optional, Premium): if you tap "Sync watch", the app asks Android's Health Connect for read-only access to steps, active calories and exercise sessions. The data is used only to show your workouts and steps inside the app, stays on your device, is never sent to anyone and is never used for ads. You can revoke access in Health Connect settings at any time.

Ads: the free version shows Google AdMob ads; Google may collect the advertising ID, approximate usage and device information under its privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy). Premium has no ads. Health data is never shared with advertisers.

Purchases: handled by Google Play; we never see payment data.

The app does not sell your data, use its own analytics or tracking, or read your messages, contacts, photos or files. Calories are estimates and do not replace a dietitian or doctor.

Deleting your data: delete entries in the app, or uninstall it to remove everything from the device.
