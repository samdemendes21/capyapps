CapyFoco privacy policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

CapyFoco is a study app with a virtual capybara. It works without an account and offline; syncing with Google is optional.

What the app stores

  • Your study sessions and logs, subjects, exams, goals, XP, streak, capybara outfits and settings.
  • Optionally, your birth date (for the capybara's birthday party) and the name you gave it.

This data stays on your device. We have no server of our own and receive none of it.

Sign in with Google to sync (optional)

If you choose to sign in with your Google account, the app keeps a copy of your study data in a hidden folder used only by CapyFoco in your own Google Drive (drive.appdata permission), so your phone and tablet stay the same.

  • The app cannot see any other file in your Drive.
  • We do not receive this data: it goes straight from your device to your Google account.
  • You can sign out at any time in Settings and delete the copy in Google Drive › Settings › Manage apps.

Ads

The free version shows Google AdMob ads. Google may collect the device advertising ID, approximate usage data and device information, under Google's privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy). You can limit or reset the advertising ID in Android settings. Premium subscribers see no ads.

Purchases

The Premium subscription is processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store payment data.

What the app does not do

  • It does not sell your data.
  • It does not use its own analytics or tracking tools.
  • It does not read your messages, contacts, photos or files.

Protected Mode (Android, optional)

If you turn on Protected Mode, the app asks for two system permissions: Usage access (to know which app is open during a session) and Display over other apps (to show the capybara on top of the apps you chose to block). This information is used only in the moment, on your device, and is never stored or sent.

In Premium, if you turn on "Silence notifications during focus", the app asks for Do Not Disturb access only to turn it on during the session and restore your previous mode when it ends. The app does not read your notifications.

Backup

  • Android may include the app's data in your Google account backup if backup is on for your device. That backup is made by Google under its own policy.
  • You can also create a backup file in Settings. The file goes wherever you choose to save or share it.

Notifications

Study reminders are scheduled on the device itself. You can turn them off in Settings.

Deleting your data

  • Uninstalling the app deletes everything from the device. Specific logs can be deleted in History, and everything can be deleted in Settings › Delete all data.
  • The synced copy can be deleted in your Google Drive (Settings › Manage apps › CapyFoco › Delete hidden app data).

Children and teens

The app is meant for students aged 13 and over. We do not collect personal data from anyone, of any age.
