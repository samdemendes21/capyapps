CapyPlants privacy policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

CapyPlants reminds you to water and fertilize your plants, with a capybara in the garden. It works without an account and offline; signing in with Google is optional.

What the app stores: the plants you add (name, place, species, watering and fertilizer schedule, pot), the watering and fertilizing you log, the capybara's name and your settings. Plant photos (Premium) are chosen by you, stay only on your device and are never sent. This data stays on your device and, if you turn on sync, in a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive. We have no server and receive none of it.

Sign in with Google, backup and sync (optional): in Settings › Backup and sync you can sign in with Google to use the app on more than one device. The app only receives your email (to show which account is connected) and the drive.appdata permission, which gives access only to a hidden CapyPlants folder in your Drive; the app cannot see or change any other file. The copy holds your plants and logs (no photos). The device talks directly to Google; nothing goes through a server of ours. To stop, tap "Sign out". To delete the copy, remove CapyPlants at myaccount.google.com › Security › Third-party apps, or delete the app's hidden data at drive.google.com › Settings › Manage apps.

Notifications: watering reminders are scheduled on the device itself.

Ads: the free version shows Google AdMob ads; Google may collect the advertising ID, approximate usage and device information under its privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy). Where the law requires it, the app asks for consent and offers "Ad privacy" in Settings. Premium has no ads.

Purchases: the Premium subscription is processed by Google Play. We never receive payment details.

What the app does not do: it does not sell your data, does not use its own analytics or tracking, and does not read your messages, contacts or files; the camera and gallery are used only when you pick a photo.

Deleting your data: plants and logs can be deleted in the app. Uninstalling removes everything from the device. The Drive copy is deleted as described above.
