CapyCare privacy policy

CapyCare — Privacy policy · last updated: Nov 19, 2026

CapyCare is made by the CapyApps studio. Contact: osamdemendes@gmail.com.

Data on your device. Goals, mood entries, tags, notes, album, streak and shop items are stored only on your phone. We have no server and cannot see this data.

Optional Google backup. If you sign in with Google, the app stores a copy of your data in CapyCare's hidden folder in your own Google Drive (appDataFolder) to sync between devices. Your email and account ID are used only for this. You can delete the backup in Settings > Backup or sign out at any time.

Ads. The free version shows Google AdMob ads, which may use your device's advertising ID. In Europe we ask for consent first. We never show ads on the mood screen or in "Get help now". Premium has no ads.

Purchases. Subscriptions are processed by Google Play; we do not receive payment details.

What we don't do. We don't sell data, we don't share your mood with anyone, and we don't run AI on your entries.

Children. The app is not directed at children under 13.

Health. CapyCare is a wellness app, not a health service, and offers no diagnosis or treatment.

Deleting data. Uninstalling removes data from the device; the Drive backup is deleted in Settings > Backup > Delete backup. Questions: osamdemendes@gmail.com.
